Tests

One row per test. Delays are percentiles over all packets (RTT from the probe; one-way delays clock-corrected, from probe and realtime UDP). Loss is the worst UDP flow per direction. Click a time to open the charts zoomed to that test.

Delay down, throughput up

Moments where the delay in a bulk direction drops to half or less (by at least 20 ms) while that direction's throughput rises by at least 20 %, holding for 3 s on each side. The kind is a heuristic from the 200 ms bins and points at where to look; it does not prove the cause.

Link pauses (both directions)

Bursts of ~120 ms stalls in uplink and downlink at the same instants, with packets held rather than lost: a device-side pause (likely dual-SIM tune-away), not queueing. Exclude these windows when judging head-of-line blocking.

One-way gap episodes

Repeated gaps (> 80 ms) in one direction only, while the other direction flows normally: packets arrive in bursts after each gap (radio scheduling or queueing in that direction).

Downloads limited on the server side

Share of a test's download time running at ≥ 200 Mbit/s with < 15 ms downlink delay. There the radio is faster than the server's own link (Wi-Fi, ~280 Mbit/s), so the queue is not on the radio and downlink head-of-line blocking is not being measured.
